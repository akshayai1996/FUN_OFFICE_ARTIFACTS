Annotation purge

Strips leftover CAD-export markers · runs entirely in this tab

What this removes — AutoCAD SHX-font placeholder markers that get carried into the PDF on DWG export. Nitro repaints them as yellow boxes on save. Every one of them is stamped with author AutoCAD SHX Text — that's the signal this tool keys on, backed by a direct yellow-color check as a fallback. Your own review markup (red lines, callouts, stamps — anything with a real author name) is never touched. Nothing leaves your browser: the file is processed locally and never uploaded anywhere.
